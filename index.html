Master's thesis

University of Verona · supervised by Prof. Daniele Meli · 2026

Causal AI Anomaly detection Explainability

Causal Anomaly Detection from Multivariate Time Series

Faults in complex systems often hide inside normal ranges: every sensor looks fine, but the relationship between them has changed. I learned causal graphs from healthy data, tracked the regression coefficients along those links with recursive least squares, and raised an alarm when they drifted. Three causal-discovery algorithms, three regression models and three real-world domains: 27 configurations under one fair pipeline.

0.81 vs 0.71

AUC-ROC on ECG data: causal detection beat the best tuned neural network.

When, not whether

Where a simple threshold already catches the faults, structure adds little. Where it fails, causal structure helps most.

9 of 9

configurations named the documented sensor in a robot attack: the explanation points at what broke, not at what moved most.

  1. Learn the causal graph
  2. Fit a healthy baseline
  3. Track coefficients with RLS
  4. Score the drift
  5. Explain the alarm
